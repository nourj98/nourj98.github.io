I am a second year PhD student in the David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science at the University of Waterloo, where I am fortunate to work under the supervision of Professor Jimmy Lin .

My research focuses on understanding how large language models can improve information access systems, studying the use of large language models across all aspects of the retrieval pipeline. I am generally interested in how large language models can be used to make information retrieval systems more effective, especially in settings where large-scale training data is unavailable. I also serve as an organizer of the TREC Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Track.

Previously, I graduated with a Master's degree from the Language Technologies Institute at Carnegie Mellon University, where I was advised by Professor Jamie Callan and Professor Eduard Hovy. After earning my Master's, I worked for two years as a researcher at MIT Lincoln Laboratory before starting my PhD.
